### Android-managed Wi-Fi bridge
- Phosh launcher `METMC Wi-Fi` queries Android Wi-Fi state and opens Android's supported Wi-Fi controls through the existing Unix-socket bridge.
- Android remains responsible for the MediaTek WLAN driver, firmware, and radio. Signal/link data is informational; no unverified transmit-power or range changes are applied.
- The bridge uses Android's user-confirmed Wi-Fi panel because ordinary apps cannot silently toggle Wi-Fi on current Android versions.

Build trigger for the integrated Wi-Fi bridge sources.

- Flatpak and Flatpak software-center integrations are purged by the Phosh setup script to reduce unnecessary rootfs packages.
